# Published data on Threads

Context numbers for sizing and metrics. Every figure is from a public source; third-party estimates are labeled.

## Scale

| Metric | Value | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly active users | 400M+ | Aug 2025 | Meta, reported by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/18/threads-edges-out-x-in-daily-mobile-users-new-data-shows/) |
| Daily active users | 150M | Oct 2025 | Meta, reported by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/18/threads-edges-out-x-in-daily-mobile-users-new-data-shows/) |
| Monthly active users | 500M | Jun 2026 | Meta announcement, reported by [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/6/threads-hits-500m-users-launches-private-feed-controls-expands-communities-and-live-chats/) |
| Mobile daily active users | Threads 141.5M vs X 125M | Jan 2026 | Similarweb estimate, via [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/18/threads-edges-out-x-in-daily-mobile-users-new-data-shows/) |
| Daily web visits | Threads 8.5M vs X 145.4M | Jan 2026 | Similarweb estimate, via [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/18/threads-edges-out-x-in-daily-mobile-users-new-data-shows/) |

**Derived:** 150M daily / 400M monthly is roughly **38%** of monthly users active on a given day. The two figures are two months apart, so treat this as approximate.

## How Meta has responded to feed complaints

| Date | Change | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 2024 | Rage-bait demotion | Instagram head said Threads would reduce reach of obvious engagement bait | [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/threads-will-look-to-reduce-the-presence-of-rage-bait-posts/729131/) |
| Nov 2024 | Follower rebalancing | Ranking shifted toward accounts people follow, away from unconnected recommendations | [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/threads-seeks-to-put-more-emphasis-on-followers-to-address-creator-concerns/733644/) |
| Jul 2025 | Hidden Words | Keyword filtering | [SocialBee changelog](https://socialbee.com/blog/threads-news/) |
| Feb 2026 | Dear Algo | Public post asking the algorithm for more or less of a topic; **lasts 3 days** | [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/meta-turned-threads-algorithm-complaints-into-an-official-feature-180000236.html) |
| Jun 2026 | Your Algo | Private topic controls for **1, 3 or 7 days** | [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/6/threads-hits-500m-users-launches-private-feed-controls-expands-communities-and-live-chats/) |
| Jun 2026 | Communities Hub | Communities out of beta with better discovery | [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/6/threads-hits-500m-users-launches-private-feed-controls-expands-communities-and-live-chats/) |

**Takeaway:** Meta has shipped at least five feed-quality changes in under two years, which signals feed relevance is a recognized priority. The two newest controls are temporary by design.
