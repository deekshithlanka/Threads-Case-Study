Meta's 2026 controls only last 1 to 7 days, so people have to keep repeating themselves. It's hard to solve because the feed has to balance personal preferences against fresh, varied content, creator reach and the risk of filter bubbles.

Who it's for

Daily Threads readers who scroll the For You feed. They want their few minutes in the app to feel worth it, not to fight the same unwanted content again.

Primary

The daily reader

Scrolls For You every day, rarely posts. Wants rejected topics and bait to stay gone.

Affected

The creator

Posts weekly to grow an audience. Wants to reach people who actually chose their topic.

At risk

The lapsing user

Used to open Threads weekly, now monthly. Needs a feed worth coming back to.

The evidence

I coded 60 public posts, forum threads and articles from 13 sources (2023 to 2026) with one shared codebook. Feed relevance was the top complaint in every source type and every year.

Competitors

Each app solves part of feed control. None makes a simple choice last.

X

Does well
For You and Following tabs, and a per-post "Not interested." Strong for real-time news.

Gap
Feedback is one post at a time and invisible, so users can't see or manage what the algorithm learned.

Bluesky

Does well
Users pin custom feeds built around topics, and those choices persist.

Gap
Users have to find or build feeds themselves, which is too much work for most people.

Threads today

Does well
Dear Algo and Your Algo let people ask for more or less of a topic in plain words.

Gap
The preferences expire after 1 to 7 days.

My solution

Threads users already tell the algorithm what they want. Dear Algo started as a user trend. The problem isn't asking, it's remembering.

"Until I change it"

A fourth duration on the controls people already use.

Smart prompt

After the same request twice in 14 days, Threads offers to keep it.

Interests page

Every saved preference in one place, with one-tap undo. Private, never shown on a profile.

Decay safeguard

A saved "less of" never fully blocks a topic, so major news still reaches people.

How we'd know

A 50/50 user-level A/B test in the five Your Algo markets, for 4 weeks so a durability effect has time to show.

North Star

Weekly Satisfied Feed Users

Users who rate at least one session that week as worth their time in an in-feed survey. It counts value, not minutes.

Test size: about 780K users per arm
