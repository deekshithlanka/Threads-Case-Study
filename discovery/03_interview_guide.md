# Interview guide

**Length:** 45 minutes. **Format:** video call, recorded with consent. **Interviewer goal:** collect specific stories about real past behavior, not opinions about hypothetical features.

## Rules for the interviewer

1. **Ask about the last time, not "usually."** "Tell me about the last time you..." gets facts. "Do you usually..." gets guesses.
2. **Do not mention the hypotheses.** Never say "feed," "algorithm," or "discovery" before the participant does. Use their words back to them.
3. **Follow energy.** If they light up or get annoyed, stay there with a probe before moving on.
4. **No pitching, no defending Threads.** If they ask what you think, say "I'm more interested in your experience."
5. **Silence is fine.** Count to 5 before rescuing a pause.
6. **Standard probes:** "Tell me more about that." "What happened next?" "Why did that matter to you?" "Can you show me?" "What did you do instead?"

Sections marked with a segment only apply to that segment. Times are targets.

---

## 0. Intro and consent (3 min)

> Thanks for making time. I'm researching how people use text-based social apps like Threads. There are no right or wrong answers, and I didn't build the app, so you won't hurt my feelings. Is it OK if I record so I can take fewer notes? The recording stays private and I'll quote you only by a participant number.

Confirm consent on the recording. Then:

> We'll talk for about 45 minutes. Near the middle I may ask you to open the app and scroll for a few minutes while you talk out loud. Any questions before we start?

## 1. Context (5 min)

1. Walk me through the apps you open on a normal day, roughly in order.
2. Which of those do you open to read what people are saying? *(Listen for: X, Bluesky, Reddit, Instagram, LinkedIn, group chats.)*
3. How did you first start using Threads? What made you try it?

## 2. Last session story (10 min)

4. Think about the last time you opened Threads. Where were you, and what made you open it?
   - Probe: Was it a notification, a habit, boredom, something specific?
5. Walk me through what you did, as best you remember.
   - Probe: What was the first thing you saw? What did you tap on?
6. How long were you on it? Why did you stop?
7. On a scale from "total waste of time" to "really glad I opened it," where was that session? Why?
   - Probe: What would have moved it one step toward "glad"?
8. Is that session typical? Tell me about a session that went differently.

## 3. Live scroll, think aloud (8 min)

> Could you open Threads and share your screen, or just describe what you see? Scroll like you normally would and tell me what goes through your head. I'll stay quiet.

Let them scroll for 3 to 4 minutes. Note every post they skip quickly, stop on, or react to. Then:

9. Of what you just saw, what did you actually care about? What did you skip, and why?
10. How did those posts end up in front of you, do you think?
11. *(If they mention unwanted content)* What have you tried to change what you see? What happened?

*Lapsed participants:* skip the live scroll. Ask instead: "Think about the last few times you used Threads before you stopped. What were those sessions like?"

## 4a. Posting and replying (posters, 8 min)

12. Tell me about the last thing you posted. What made you post it?
13. What happened after you posted? How did you feel about the response?
    - Probe: How do you decide if a post "worked"?
14. Is there something you thought about posting recently but didn't? What stopped you?
15. Where else do you post similar things? How does the response compare?

## 4b. Reading without posting (readers, 8 min)

12. When was the last time you thought about posting or replying but didn't? What stopped you?
13. What would have to be true for you to post more?
14. What do you get out of reading without posting?

## 4c. Leaving (lapsed, 8 min)

12. Think back to when you stopped opening Threads. Was there a moment, or did it fade out?
13. What were you doing on Threads before you stopped? What replaced that time?
14. What would have to change for you to come back? *(Then ask: Has anything made you consider it?)*

## 5. Alternatives (6 min)

16. When you want to know what people are saying about something happening right now, where do you go first? Why there?
17. *(If they use X, Bluesky or Reddit)* What does that app do for you that Threads doesn't? And the reverse?
18. Have you recommended Threads to anyone, or told anyone not to bother? What did you say?

## 6. Wrap up (5 min)

19. If you could change one thing about Threads, what would it be? Why that one?
20. Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?

> Thank you. This was really helpful. Would it be OK to follow up with a quick question by email if something comes up?
